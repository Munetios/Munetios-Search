Privacy Policy

historyLast updated: November 21, 2026

This policy describes how Munetios handles information when you use Search, Munetios Accounts, and related Munetios apps. The information involved depends on the features you use.

Search and external services

Search, previews, and media can send requests directly to an external provider or through Munetios infrastructure. Depending on the request, a provider may receive a search query or other information needed to return a result, plus technical information such as an IP address and browser headers. A provider’s own privacy policy governs how it handles that information.

Sources for Search may include Wikipedia, GitHub, and DuckDuckGo Instant Answers. External resources may also supply favicons, images, maps, or fonts. Browser and operating system providers may process audio when you use voice input. Optional AI features may send prompts and any material you submit to the service used to generate the result.

Munetios uses information to deliver the features you request, maintain secure accounts, and prevent abuse. Munetios does not sell personal information or use it for behavioral advertising.

Munetios Accounts

When you create an account, we process your email address and a password hash if you use a password. We also process session and device details needed to manage sign-ins and protect your account. The account can store an optional display name, profile photo, and verified recovery email address.

If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier and verified email address. We may use the name and photo Google provides to set up your Munetios profile. Email verification and account recovery use the addresses you supply so we can deliver codes and account messages.

You can edit your account details, manage devices, and choose whether other people can see your profile picture under Account → Data & privacy. You can still see your profile picture when you turn off sharing.

Documents, sharing, and mail

OmniWrite can store documents on your device. When you enable encrypted sync, Munetios stores encrypted copies. Munetios can read files shared with anyone who has the link. Sharing also makes information such as your display name and permitted profile picture visible to people with access.

Munetios Mail processes sender and recipient addresses and message content to send mail, route incoming messages, and display your mailbox. Mail providers also process the messages they send or receive.

Managed classrooms and devices

On managed devices, a school or organization can use Munetios WebFilter to manage browsing rules and device access. Authorized staff may see device information, open tabs, and screen views through the features their organization enables. Ask the organization that manages your device about its use of those features.

Cookies and browser storage

Munetios uses session cookies to keep you signed in. Browser storage can hold preferences, local documents, and other data used by apps. See the Cookies Policy for details.

Your choices and privacy requests

Account settings let you change your information, sign out devices, export local app data, or request account deletion. Manage data stored only on your device through the app or your browser settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, and complain to a data protection authority. To make a request, contact munetios96@gmail.com. If you believe a child’s personal information was provided to Munetios, use the same address to contact us.
